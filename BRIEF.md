# Business Brief: MTD Checker

**Who:** UK self-employed people and landlords with income roughly £15k-£60k who are unsure whether/when HMRC's Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax rules apply to them.

**What:** A free, fast web tool — enter your self-employment/property income, get back the exact date MTD affects you, what digital record-keeping software you'll need by then, and an optional email reminder as your deadline approaches.

**Why they'd use it / pay:** GOV.UK has an official checker but it's generic, doesn't personalize timelines across multiple income sources cleanly, and doesn't help with the "what do I do now" follow-up (software choice). MTD thresholds roll out in stages (£50k from Apr 2026, £30k from Apr 2027, £20k from Apr 2028), so this is a multi-year wave of fresh, recurring search demand, not a one-off.

**How money is made:** Affiliate commissions from UK accounting software referral programs (FreeAgent, QuickBooks UK, Xero, Coconut) recommended based on the user's situation. Secondary: email list for a later small one-off "MTD compliance checklist" product once the audience and trust are established.

**Riskiest assumption:** That people actually search for this *now*, ahead of their own threshold year, rather than waiting until weeks before their deadline (which would mean a 1-2 year wait for traffic to ramp for the lower thresholds). To be validated in Phase 1 with real keyword/search-trend data before heavy build-out.

**Status:** Niche selected 2026-06-25. Next: cheap validation (landing page + keyword check) before building the full personalization logic.
