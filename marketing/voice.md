# Brand Voice — MTD Checker

## Tone
Plain, helpful, slightly dry. Sounds like a knowledgeable peer explaining a tax-admin thing simply over a coffee — not a marketer, not a comedian, not HMRC's press office.

## Always
- Disclose the affiliate relationship whenever recommending software we earn commission from ("disclosure: affiliate link" or equivalent, per ASA/CAP UK rules).
- Be factually accurate about MTD thresholds/dates/penalties — cite GOV.UK where it matters.
- Sound like a real person who happens to have built a small tool, not a brand account.
- Keep penalty/deadline information factual and reassuring, not scary. The goal is to reduce anxiety with clarity, never to manufacture urgency.

## Never
- Never imply we're qualified accountants or registered tax advisors. We built a calculator; we are not giving personal tax advice. If a question requires real advice, say so and suggest an accountant.
- Never take a position on HMRC, tax policy, or politics. Stay neutral and practical — strong opinions here are pure downside.
- Never post identical copy across communities. Every post is written for that specific community's norms.
- Never claim the tool is "official" or affiliated with HMRC.
- Never use urgency/fear language about penalties to drive clicks.

## Platform-specific notes
- **Reddit:** drier, more detailed, answer the actual question first — the link (if any) is secondary and only included where it adds real value and the subreddit's rules allow it.
- **TikTok:** same underlying facts, punchier delivery, can be a bit more casual/conversational, but the "never" list still applies in full — no fear-mongering hooks, no fake urgency, no implying official status.

## Disclosure language (use as-is or adapt minimally)
- Reddit comment mentioning the tool: "(disclosure: I built this, free to use, no signup)"
- Reddit comment mentioning affiliate software: "(heads up, some of the software links on that page are affiliate links — doesn't cost you anything extra)"
- TikTok caption: "Link in bio. I built this tool — free, no login. Some software links on the site are affiliate links."
